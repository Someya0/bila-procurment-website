OUR HERITAGE

Precision in Procurement, Locally Rooted.

Established in 2015, Bila Procurement and Logistics was born from a vision to redefine how essential services and goods are delivered in Mogadishu. In a rapidly evolving commercial landscape, we serve as the critical link for NGOs and commercial enterprises requiring uncompromising reliability.

Our presence in Mogadishu isn’t just about office space; it’s about deep-seated community ties and an intricate understanding of the local logistical challenges. We combine international best practices with on-the-ground agility to ensure every contract is executed with surgical precision.
